# L Stop

L Stop is a free daily game by James Robinson. Guess the day's station on the L, Chicago's train system, in six tries. Play it at https://griffinley.com/james-robinson/

A $3 practice version at https://payhip.com/b/na86d plays random stations, or any past day by its number.

Anyone can copy it and make one for their own city's trains. The stations are in one file, stations.js. It has each station's name, its lines, its place on the map and the order of stops on each line.

## Changing the city

1. Put your stations in stations.js, spelling each name the same every time.
2. Then paste it over the old list at the top of index.html, the game's page, and keep the CITY block above it.
3. In the CITY block, start holds the first day's date. Change it to yours.
4. Put your own web address in page, which is what the Share button copies.
5. Change America/Chicago to your time zone, and the words on the page that say Chicago.

## Data

Station names, lines and coordinates are facts read on Wikipedia's list of Chicago L stations, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Chicago_%22L%22_stations . Not connected with the Chicago Transit Authority.
