L Stop

A free daily game by James Robinson. Published October 10, 2026

Guess today's Chicago L station in six tries. The L is Chicago's train system, eight lines named by color. After each guess you see which train lines stop at both stations, and how many stops apart they are.

How it works Type a station name and press the Guess button. Each row names the train lines (Red, Blue, Brown and the rest) that stop at both your guess and today's station. It also tells you how many stops apart the two stations are by the shortest ride, even when that ride means changing trains. Stops are counted along the regular trains, so the Purple Line express, a rush-hour train that skips most stops, is left out. A new station starts at midnight Chicago time.

    When the game ends, a Share button copies your result as a row of squares to paste anywhere. A yellow square is a guess that shared a line with today's station and a white one didn't.

    The map marks each guess with a numbered dot, 1 for your first guess, 2 for your second. Today's station is marked too once the game ends.

    Station names and lines come from Wikipedia's list of Chicago L stations. Not connected with the Chicago Transit Authority.
